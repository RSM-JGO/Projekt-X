## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished, original, premium logo for an IT project-management web application named exactly "Projekt X". Brand context: modern professional SaaS for project planning, tasks, milestones and management reporting; user likes modern pastel accents and both light and dark UI. Produce ONE finished horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, not a presentation board. Symbol on the left: an exceptionally clean and memorable abstract X constructed from interlocking geometric ribbon-like segments with subtly rounded ends; clever negative space suggests coordinated paths converging and moving forward. Balanced, confident, distinctive silhouette, readable as an app icon at small size. Palette: pastel lavender, periwinkle blue, restrained mint accents with sufficient saturation for contrast on both white and deep charcoal surfaces. Wordmark on the right: exact text "Projekt X", sophisticated contemporary geometric sans-serif, medium-bold weight, excellent kerning and generous clear space; use a rich medium periwinkle-violet color that remains visible on both light and dark backgrounds. Flat vector-style graphic, crisp precise edges, professional brand-design craftsmanship. Icon and wordmark horizontally aligned and optically balanced. Logo should occupy most of the image with generous safe margins. No extra text, no slogan, no gradients into white, no glow, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup objects, no sample background tiles, no watermark, no generic rocket/checkmark/briefcase graphics. True transparent alpha background.
